# Privacy Policy — YoYo Player

YoYo Player does not collect, send or sell any data.

- **No tracking or analytics.** The extension makes no network requests.
- **What it stores:** your settings (the two automatic-mode switches) and the floating player's last position and size. This is saved with the browser's extension storage on your device. If browser sync is turned on, the browser may sync your settings between your devices.
- **What it reads:** on the page you're viewing, it looks for video elements and the page title (shown in the player). Nothing leaves your browser.
- **YoYo Companion (optional Mac app):** it uses the Screen Recording permission only to read the browser's pop-out window and show a copy of it on screen. Frames aren't saved or sent anywhere. It remembers only where you last placed the copy and whether the permission is on. The extension talks to it only on your Mac, to start it, stop it and show its status.
- **Why it runs on all sites:** so it can find videos and float them on any site you use, including automatically when you scroll or switch tabs.

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

Questions: contact the developer through the extension's store listing.
